    Welcome to NHSNLink Onboarding
    
        
    The National Healthcare Safety Network (NHSN) uses NHSNLink, an open-source Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR®) application, to enable reporting of digital quality measures (dQMs) to NHSN. The enrollment information populated in this web form supports secure and successful authentication of NHSNLink with your facility’s FHIR endpoint, adequate understanding by NHSN of your FHIR data submissions, and successful reporting of dQMs to NHSN.

    Epic Patient Lists
    
                  
    NHSNLink uses the Epic FHIR List API to retrieve patient IDs from system patient lists, so that
                  admitted and recently admitted patient lists can be queried daily. This provides a historical,
                  complete list of FHIR Patient IDs needed for scheduled reporting. The Epic List API has a limit of 1500 patients returned with any one query, so building six separate system lists is recommended to keep daily queries well under the 1500 limit threshold and to avoid missing patients with short admissions.

    Location Organization Resolution
    
        
    Often reporting facilities within a health system will share a common FHIR endpoint with other facilities in the network. Due to query limitations from EHR (Electronic Health Record) systems, there's a risk that NHSNLink may retrieve patient related FHIR resources that don't belong to the reporting facility. To address this, configuration on specific FHIR Location values must be provided to ensure that any patient Encounters that are evaluated for NHSN measures are within the reporting facility. No configuration is needed unless your facility's FHIR data is shared with other facilities in your health system.
    

        
    
          
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Cerner Millennium supports querying Locations by physical type. NHSNLink can use this capability to search for Locations tagged as "Site" which typically can be used to correlate Location resources that represent your reporting facility organization. The search button below can assist in applying these mappings.

    Encounter Mapping
    
        
    The NHSN measures evaluated by Link conform to the FHIR US Core implementation guide. This guide is a set of rules and best practices that help healthcare providers share patient information accross the United States. In this rule set, all patient FHIR Encounters must have an Encounter Type (e.g. e-mail consultation, surgical day-care, skilled nursing, rehabilitation) that maps to Patient Encounter Procedure SNOMED or CPT codes. More information on those requirements can be found here. Below is a form to associate your local Encounter.type codes to their corresponding SMOMED or CPT codes.
